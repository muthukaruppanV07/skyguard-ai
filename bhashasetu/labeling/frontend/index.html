    ISL grammar cheat sheet
    • OSV order: Object → Subject → Verb (card-ration mine need)
    • No copula, no articles (fever I = "I have a fever")
    • Fingerspell proper nouns (A-D-H-A-A-R)
    • 2-handed signs are the norm; weak hand = placement cue
    • Questions: raised brows (yes/no), furrowed + wh-sign
